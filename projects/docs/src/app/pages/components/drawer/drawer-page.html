Drawer

A drawer holds a panel the user opens from a page and leaves again: a device list, settings, a filter. It is modal like a dialog: the page behind it is dimmed and inert, focus stays inside, and Escape closes it. On a tablet or desktop it is a full-height panel that slides in from the side. On a phone it becomes the same bottom sheet as a dialog, as tall as its content, that can be swiped away. Open one with CkDrawer from @ckui/core/drawer and a component or template, or declare a <ck-drawer> in a template. The content is written with the dialog's building blocks.

Examples

Service: a component with data and a result

open() takes a component or a template and returns a CkDrawerRef, which is the ref of CkDialog. The content injects its data with CK_DRAWER_DATA and the ref with CkDrawerRef. ref.closed resolves after the drawer has left the screen, with the value passed to close() or [ckDialogClose], and with undefined when the drawer was dismissed.

Template: ck-drawer

<ck-drawer> declares the same drawer in a template. Its content is one <ng-template>, rendered only while open is true, so nothing in it exists while the drawer is closed. [(open)] follows the drawer back to false whenever it closes, and (closed) carries the result.

Start and end

side picks the edge. end, the default, is the right edge in a left-to-right page and the left edge in a right-to-left one; start is the other. The panel slides in from its edge, fills the height of the screen and keeps its content out of display cut-outs. Only the body scrolls when the content is taller than the screen. A side panel has no swipe: close it with Escape, the backdrop or the close button.

Side panel or sheet

mode: 'auto', the default, opens a sheet when the screen is narrow and the pointer is coarse, and a side panel otherwise. It is decided once, when the drawer opens. A narrow window with a mouse keeps the side panel, capped at the width of the screen minus a margin. Force side or sheet when the content needs one shape. fullHeight makes a sheet as tall as the screen allows.

Providers of the opener

A component opened through CkDrawer sees the providers of the app root. For providers that live lower, such as route-level ones or those of the opening component, pass inject(Injector) from the opener as injector. CK_DRAWER_DATA and CkDrawerRef are provided on top of it. A template keeps the injector of the place where it was declared.

Closing again does nothing: only the first close() counts. When the app is destroyed every open drawer is removed, the page behind it is live again and every closed resolves with undefined. On the server nothing is rendered. The <ck-drawer> inputs are read when the drawer opens; changing one while it is open has no effect. Do not confuse the <ck-drawer> element, which only declares the drawer and renders nothing itself, with the .ck-drawer-panel class of the surface in the overlay. A drawer that has neither a ckDialogTitle nor an ariaLabel logs a warning in development.

Accessibility

  • The panel has role="dialog" or alertdialog and aria-modal="true". ckDialogTitle names it and ckDialogDescription describes it; without a title, set ariaLabel. The names follow the content: a title that appears later or goes away updates the panel.
  • Initial focus goes to the element with ckDialogInitialFocus. Without one it goes to the first tabbable element of the content, never the close button, which comes last in the tab order. A drawer with nothing to press focuses the panel itself.
  • Focus is trapped while the drawer is open and the page behind it is inert, so neither Tab nor a screen reader's virtual cursor leaves. When it closes, focus returns to the element that opened it. If the opener is gone, move focus yourself after ref.closed resolves, to a sensible place such as the list the opener belonged to.
  • A dialog opened from a drawer, for example a confirmation, sits above it. The drawer is inert while it is open, Escape closes only the dialog, and focus goes back into the drawer.
  • Escape closes only the topmost drawer. A drawer with dismissible: false ignores Escape, the backdrop and the swipe; make sure it always has a button that closes it.
  • The close button is named by the close label, which can be translated through provideCkui({ labels }), and is at least 44px on a touch screen. On a sheet the swipe handle is decorative; every action it offers also exists as a button.
  • A phone gets the sheet: the actions stack at full width in reading order and the sheet keeps the safe-area inset of the screen below its content. A side panel keeps its content out of the cut-outs at the top, the bottom and its own edge. The page behind cannot scroll. A right-to-left page puts end on the left.
  • With prefers-reduced-motion the drawer neither slides nor fades. In forced-colors mode the panel has a visible border.
  • Global element styles. A reset such as h2 { margin: … }, p { margin: … } or button:focus-visible { outline: … } beats the layered drawer styles, and a rule that sets display on the host element of a drawer component stops its body from scrolling. Declare resets in a layer before ckui, or scope them with :not(.ck-dialog__title), :not(.ck-dialog__description) and :not(.ck-button).
  • The drawer lives in the overlay container, a child of body. Under a nested data-ck-theme it follows the theme of the page, not of the section that opened it.
