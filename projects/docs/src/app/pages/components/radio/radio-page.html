Radio

ck-radio-group picks one option out of a few that are all visible, each a ck-radio: a weight unit, a view, a plan. The group holds the value, the radios are native <input type="radio"> inputs under labels, so the whole row is the tap target and the arrow keys, Tab and Space come from the browser. It works with Reactive Forms, ngModel and Signal Forms. Import CK_RADIO_IMPORTS (the group, the radio, ck-hint and ck-error) from @ckui/core/radio. For two or three short options that act like a toggle bar, a segmented control is more compact; for a list of many options, use a select.

Examples

Basics

The value is the option of the checked radio and works two-way with [(value)]. The first <label> names the group (role="radiogroup"). The text inside ck-radio names the radio, and a ck-hint inside it describes that radio; a ck-hint that is a direct child of the group describes the whole group. A click anywhere on a row checks it, and the hint is part of the row.

A required choice

Nothing is checked until the user picks: null is "no answer", and Validators.required fails it. A ck-error stays hidden until the control is invalid and touched, or its form was submitted. Touched means focus left the whole group, not that it moved between the radios. While the error shows, every input is aria-invalid and the error is part of the group's description, so a screen reader reads it when focus enters the group. required on the group draws the marker on the label and sets aria-required on the radiogroup; validation is your form's job.

Sizes

The size follows provideCkui() unless the group sets it, and every radio takes the group's size. The circle grows from 1.125rem to 1.5rem, and the row is never lower than the control height of its size, so it is 44px or more on a coarse pointer and 60px at xl.

Disabled

A disabled radio is blocked, greyed and skipped by the arrow keys, and it keeps showing its state by the dot. A disabled group disables every radio in it, and a <fieldset disabled> around a group does the same without any input.

The group gets the classes ck-radio-group, ck-radio-group--{size} and, while they apply, ck-radio-group--inline and ck-radio-group--disabled. A radio outside a group, a standalone radio, a card or tile variant and a radio inside ck-field are not part of this component.

Accessibility

  • It follows the WAI-ARIA radio group pattern with native <input type="radio"> inputs inside a role="radiogroup" named by its label. The text names each radio and a ck-hint describes it; the name does not repeat the hint. aria-required is set on the group when it is required.
  • The state is never colour alone: the checked circle is filled and holds a dot, and the border of the unchecked circle is at least 3:1 from the surface, in both shipped themes.
  • Errors: while the group shows its error, every input is aria-invalid="true" and the error is in the group's aria-describedby. A radiogroup is not shown with aria-invalid itself, so each radio carries it. The error appears after focus left the group or the form was submitted, never while the user is still on the first pass.
  • The row is the tap target: at least the control height of the size, so 44px or more on a coarse pointer and 60px at xl. A long label wraps in its own block, with the circle next to its first line.
  • The focus ring is drawn around the circle for keyboard focus only. In forced-colors mode the circle's border is CanvasText, the focus ring is Highlight, a checked circle is Highlight with a HighlightText dot, and a disabled one is GrayText.
  • The dot scales in over --ck-duration-normal, and not at all under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Linting. angular-eslint's label-has-associated-control flags the <label> that names a group, because it labels no control by for. Turn the rule off for templates that use ck-radio-group, or name the group with aria-label.
  • Global element styles. The parts are a plain label and input. An unlayered rule such as input { border: … } beats the layered control. Keep those rules off the radio (input:not(.ck-radio__input)) or put resets in a layer declared before ckui. The first rule below really breaks the radio; the rest scope it.
