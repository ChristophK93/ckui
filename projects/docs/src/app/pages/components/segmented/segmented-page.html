Segmented

ck-segmented is a short row of mutually exclusive options, such as Day, Week and Month. It is one radio group: one tab stop, and the arrow keys move and select. It works with Reactive Forms, ngModel and Signal Forms. Import CK_SEGMENTED_IMPORTS from @ckui/core/segmented.

No selection

A value of null, or one no segment has, selects nothing: no segment is checked and the indicator is hidden. Tab still enters on the first enabled segment, and focusing a segment does not select it.

One control, three form APIs

The same control three times. A programmatic write (setValue, a model change, reset) moves the indicator and never counts as a change by the user; only a click or a key does.

Sizes

The size follows provideCkui() unless you set it. The height is the control height of the size, so the group lines up with a button or an input of the same size, and each segment is that tall to hit. Under a coarse pointer sm and md are 44px high; xl is 60px.

Icons

An svg that is a direct child of a segment is its icon. It is drawn before the text and sized to the font; give it aria-hidden="true". Any other element, such as a span with an icon font, lands in the label next to the text and is cut like text. A segment with only an icon needs an aria-label: development builds warn without one.

Long labels

A segment is as wide as an equal share of the group or its label with icon and padding, whichever is larger. A long label makes its own column wider and the others narrower, so nothing is cut as long as the labels fit together. The group below sits in a 328px container, the content width of a phone at 360px with a 16px page gutter. Only when the labels cannot fit together at all, as in the second group, does the group switch to equal columns and cut them with an ellipsis. The full text stays in the page, so the accessible name is not shortened. The group goes back to content widths as soon as there is room again, and it never grows wider than its container. --ck-segmented-padding-inline gives a tighter control more room.

Disabled

A disabled segment stays visible, cannot be selected and is skipped by the arrow keys. A disabled group leaves the tab order, sets aria-disabled on the group and every segment, and ignores clicks and keys.

Accessibility

  • The group follows the WAI-ARIA radio group pattern: role="radiogroup" on the group, role="radio" with aria-checked on each segment, and a roving tab index, so the whole group is one tab stop. Arrow keys move focus and select, as on native radio buttons.
  • The group needs a name (aria-label or aria-labelledby). A segment is named by its text; an icon-only segment needs an aria-label. Development builds warn about both.
  • The selected segment is not marked by colour alone: the indicator is a raised pill with its own 1px border (3:1 against the track) and a shadow. In forced-colors mode it is Highlight with HighlightText and the track keeps a visible border.
  • Every segment is as tall as the track (at least 44px on a coarse pointer, 60px at xl), including the strip around the indicator.
  • In right-to-left text the segments run right to left, the indicator mirrors, and ArrowLeft and ArrowRight swap. The direction comes from the dir attribute on the group or an ancestor, not from the CSS direction property.
  • The indicator sits in the grid area of the selected segment, so any column width works and server-rendered markup is right without script. It slides to a new selection over --ck-duration-normal, and not at all under prefers-reduced-motion. It appears in place the first time a value is set.
  • A disabled group or segment sets aria-disabled and leaves the tab order; the colour change is not the only cue.
  • A theme whose --ck-color-control-border is transparent must give the indicator a contrasting fill or border of its own (3:1 against the track), for example with --ck-segmented-indicator-bg, or the selected segment is marked by a faint fill only.
  • The focus ring is drawn inside the segment on keyboard focus only, in forced-colors mode too. The hover fill shows on unselected segments with a hovering pointer only.
  • Global element styles. A global rule such as :focus-visible { outline: … } beats the layered control and draws a second ring on the segment itself, which this site's own rule does too (the demos switch it off). Keep it off the segments or put resets in a layer declared before ckui.
